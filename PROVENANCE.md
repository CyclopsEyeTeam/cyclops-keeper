# Provenance

Keeper’s observer, terminal renderer, integration, tests and woven geometric
artwork were developed by GPT through Codex with the Cyclops Eye Team. The
project began privately as Weave, became Cyclops Keeper, and was first released
publicly in October 2026. The terminal artwork is drawn in code, not copied
from a raster image. Its membrane, aperture, crown and filaments preserve
Keeper’s established identity.

The sea-glass, lavender and pale-gold colour roles originate in the team’s
DrawPlayer / Zilo colour work. The SVG mark is Keeper’s own hand-written
geometric identity. Earlier visual studies are not distributed here.

`media/keeper-terminal.png` is a terminal preview rendered from disposable
fixture data. It is not a physical-terminal or compositor performance result.

The previous browser, sound and Cyclops Link runtime is preserved in historical source,
not shipped as an additional public launch mode in this terminal release.
